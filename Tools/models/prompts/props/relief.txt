A carved cream limestone Buddhist relief panel, a rectangular stone slab about 1.2 m wide and 1.6 m tall, in the style of the Longmen Grottoes with Gandhara influence. In the centre a deep arched niche containing a seated Buddha in meditation (lotus position, hands in dhyana mudra, serene face with downcast eyes, ushnisha with snail-shell curls, long earlobes, robe with flowing parallel carved folds), seated on a lotus throne. Behind his head and body a large pointed flame-shaped halo (mandorla) carved with radiating flame tongues and small seated Buddhas. On each side of the niche a standing bodhisattva attendant in high relief with jewellery and flowing scarves, and above them flying apsaras with ribbons. A carved border of lotus scrolls frames the panel. Deep, crisp carving with real depth and strong shadows. Weathered warm cream stone with subtle ochre staining. Straight-on frontal view, flat orthographic, the whole panel visible edge to edge, nothing cropped, plain light grey background, soft raking light from the upper left, photorealistic, no text.
